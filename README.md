# Christmas dinner timetable

A single-page planner for cooking Christmas dinner, built from our family timetable.

- **Run-sheet**: every job from the day before to serving, with tick boxes
- **On the menu**: each dish with its oven, temperature and cooking window
- **Two ovens and a hob**: a chart of what is cooking where through the afternoon, with a warning when an oven gets crowded
- **Running late**: change the serving time, or push every unticked job back 10 minutes
- **Alarms**: a chime and an on-screen card when each job is due (tap "Alarms" each time you open the page)
- **Kitchen mode**: one job at a time in big type, with Done, Back and Skip ahead
- **Practice run**: drag the slider to rehearse the day

Ticks and delays are saved in your own browser only, so they don't sync between devices.

Everything is in `index.html`: no build step, no dependencies beyond Google Fonts.
